# CypherTube v1.0 Wiki

## Architectural Overview
The CypherTube OS is built on the Holland Core, utilizing a Sovereign Organism architecture.

### Sovereign Space
All interaction occurs within the **Canonical Sovereign Space (CSS)**, a normalized 3D coordinate system.

### Persistence (.ctube)
State is persisted in signed binary files to ensure integrity across reboots.

### Ritual Grammar
Interaction is driven by multimodal fusion of voice, gaze, and gesture, resolved into system intents.

### Governance
Governance is handled via the LedgerConsensusGovernor and validated by the Sentinel Protocol Gate.
